HOW IT WORKS

From first draft to a lasting record.

  1. Create a project

    Name it and pick your discipline: music, design, writing, film or software.

  2. Fingerprint each version

    Choose a file and its SHA-256 fingerprint is computed in your browser. Only the fingerprint and its details are saved, with the time set by ProofStream's server. The file never leaves your device.

  3. Check it any time

    Drop the same file in later: a matching fingerprint shows it is byte-for-byte the version you saved. Export your timeline whenever you need a copy.

FOR RECORDINGS

ISRCs, splits and an on-chain record.

Keep the facts about a recording in one place, with every change on the record.

  • Your ISRC and your splits. Record the ISRC you already have, with the split for every role and territory, checked to add up to 100%.
  • Verification with evidence. Ask ProofStream to verify a claim with your split sheet or distributor statement. Evidence is fingerprinted on your device, never uploaded.

  • History that stays. A correction becomes a new version. Earlier versions are never rewritten.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is ProofStream free?

Yes, during this preview. You can create an account with your email or Google and use the workspace at no charge. ProofStream does not collect royalties or take payments. If we ever introduce paid plans, we will tell you clearly before anything is charged.

Are my files uploaded?

No. When you fingerprint a draft, your browser computes a SHA-256 hash of the file on your own device. The file never leaves your device. We store only the fingerprint (a fixed-length string that cannot be turned back into your file), the time set by ProofStream's server, and the details you choose to enter. Evidence you use for a verification request is also fingerprinted on your device, not uploaded.

What is a fingerprint?

A fingerprint is a SHA-256 hash of a file, computed in your browser. It is a unique string for that exact sequence of bytes. If you later drop in the same file, a matching fingerprint shows it is byte-for-byte the version you saved. Change even a little, and the fingerprint changes. A fingerprint identifies a file. It does not describe the file's contents, and it cannot be reversed to rebuild the file.

Is this proof of legal ownership or copyright?

No. A fingerprint or a timestamp is not proof of legal ownership or copyright. ProofStream is a record-keeping tool. It does not register copyright, give legal advice, or decide who owns a work. If you need legal protection, talk to a qualified lawyer. You remain responsible for the rights in the work you fingerprint and the claims you record.

Does ProofStream issue ISRCs?

No. SoundExchange and IFPI-authorised agencies issue ISRCs. ProofStream only records the ISRCs you already have, along with splits, roles and territories you enter, and checks that the splits add up to 100%. You are responsible for the accuracy of that information. ProofStream does not assign, generate or sell ISRCs, and it does not bundle UPCs.

What does “on-chain” mean, and what is Sepolia?

When a ProofStream administrator verifies a version of a recording claim, ProofStream writes a cryptographic hash of that claim's canonical record to a public smart contract (the ProofStreamRegistry). The anchor is a hash, not your file, your name or your email. Blockchain records are public and permanent. Once a hash is anchored, it cannot be changed or deleted, including if you later delete your account.

While ProofStream is in preview, this anchoring runs on Ethereum's Sepolia test network, not on Ethereum mainnet. A test network is public and experimental. If you do not want a claim anchored, do not request its verification.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it, or use the account-deletion option in the workspace if it is available. When we delete your account, we delete your registration, projects, sign-in identities, links and recording claims from our database. Backups roll off on our provider's normal schedule. We may keep limited records where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes, prevent fraud and enforce our terms.

On-chain anchors cannot be deleted. If you do not want a claim written to a public blockchain, do not request its verification.
